Shock Check

A twelve month game that shows what one bad month does to your savings.

How it works

Saved scenarios and share links live in your browser. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded.

Median U.S. rent went from $602 in 2000 to $1,475 in 2024, about 3.8% a year, so the 10% rent hike is a fair test.

Each game deals six random surprises into twelve months. Credit card debt grows 2% a month.

The three lives and the surprise costs are made-up examples. No taxes, family help or public programs.
